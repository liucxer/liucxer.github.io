Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-09

PreOwned Islands does not collect any data. Period. No exceptions.

Specifically

  • The app runs fully offline. It never connects to any server we operate.
  • Your saved islands, your pretend-bid history, and your nickname are stored exclusively on your device via standard NSUserDefaults. They are not synced, uploaded, or backed up to anything we control.
  • The app contains no analytics framework (no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry, Crashlytics, Firebase).
  • The app contains no advertising framework.
  • The app contains no third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • The app makes no network requests of its own.
  • No account, no email, no phone number.

About the map

The app uses Apple's MapKit framework to display satellite tiles. When you open the map, iOS may cache Apple's own map data — that's handled by Apple and we have no visibility into it. We never report to Apple, to ourselves, or to any third party which islands you viewed.

About the "Place Pretend Bid" feature

"Place Pretend Bid" is a fully local entertainment feature. Whatever amount you input, whatever nickname you enter, lives only in your device's app storage. There is no server. There is no recipient. There is no transaction.

The bid confirmation slip is rendered by SwiftUI on your device. You can screenshot, save, and share it. We don't see it, transmit it, or store it anywhere outside your device.

About the island data

The 30+ island listings shipped in the app are anonymized synthetic data based on patterns observed in public broker listings worldwide. Names, exact coordinates, and prices have been blurred. No specific real listing is directly reproduced. This protects third-party broker data and avoids any inadvertent disclosure of real seller information.

The app does not scrape, copy, or store any broker site's original photographs or descriptions. All in-app visuals are produced by the app itself (SF Symbols + linear gradients), and the map content is Apple's MapKit.

Permissions

The app requests no system permissions — no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no photos, no notifications.

Children

App Store rating 4+, but the subject matter is high-value-asset browsing aimed at adult audiences. We collect no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Apple's policies still apply

Like any App Store download, your acquisition of PreOwned Islands is governed by Apple's privacy policy and App Store terms. The developer (Changxi Liu) does not have visibility into who has purchased the app — only aggregate sales counts.

Disclaimer

This app is for browsing and entertainment only. We do not facilitate any transactions and do not represent any broker. Real island acquisition requires qualified legal counsel, financial advisors, and specialist real-estate brokers.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's "What's New" release notes.
