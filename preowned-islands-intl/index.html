PreOwned Islands

Previously owned. Painfully expensive.

The world has Pre-Owned Rolex stores. Pre-Owned Hermès. Pre-Owned Bentley.

Why not Pre-Owned Islands?

This is the first iPhone app dedicated to browsing the second-hand market for private islands worldwide — listings from the Caribbean to the South Pacific, the Outer Hebrides to the Coromandel — each with its own provenance, sale history, and the dryly amusing reason the current owner is parting with it.

We are not brokers. We do not facilitate transactions. This is, in fact, just a very strange catalogue.

"Caribbean Cinco · 12.4 hectares
Previous owner: someone in technology.
Asking: $4,200,000. Sold last in 2017 for $3,100,000.
Per the broker, parting because they have 'acquired something larger'
and because 'passing fishing vessels turned out to be louder than anticipated.'"

What it does

📋
30+ curated listings across 7 regions Caribbean, Pacific, Southeast Asia, Europe, North America, Oceania, Africa. Each with size, asking price, distance from coast, provenance narrative, and where applicable, previous sale data.
🗺
Global satellite map Every island pinned on MapKit. Tap a pin to see the listing detail. Coordinates intentionally blurred — region only.
🏷
New / Pre-Owned / Reduced / Sold filters Like a used-car lot, but for the literal opposite of a used car. The "Sold" archive lets you see what comparable islands actually changed hands for.
💼
Place Pretend Bid Pick a number. The app generates a dignified-looking bid confirmation slip with your name, the lot, and your fabricated offer. Screenshot and share. We don't tell the seller. There is no seller.
🔖
Private wishlist Save your favorites locally. Return to them. Pretend you're considering. We will not judge.

Design principles

  • Offline. Listings ship inside the app. Browse on a plane.
  • Zero collection. No account, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no upload of any kind.
  • One-time price. No subscription, no IAP, no ads.
  • Not a marketplace. We do not represent or contact any broker. This is a catalogue and an entertainment product.
  • Anonymized data. All listings are synthetic; coordinates are blurred. We don't reproduce any real broker's listing verbatim. See Privacy.
