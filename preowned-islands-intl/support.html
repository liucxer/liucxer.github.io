Frequently asked questions

Are these real islands?

They are based on real listings on the global private-island market — but the entries in this app are anonymized synthetic data. Names, exact coordinates, and prices have been blurred. No specific real listing is reproduced verbatim. This is by design — it lets us be a catalogue without infringing on any broker's content.

Can I actually buy an island in the app?

No. This app is for browsing and entertainment only. We do not facilitate any transactions and do not represent any broker. For real island acquisition, you'll need a qualified real-estate attorney, a broker who specializes in international waterfront, and a meeting that's nothing like this app.

Does "Place Pretend Bid" really send a bid to anyone?

No, and no one is on the other end. It's a local entertainment feature. The app generates a bid confirmation slip you can screenshot and share. All bid data stays on your device — even we can't see it.

How do I switch between New / Pre-Owned / Reduced / Sold?

From the Browse tab, the chips at the top filter by status. "Sold" is an archive — it shows listings that have closed, with their actual sale price, useful as a market comp.

Why are the map coordinates not exact?

Coordinates are intentionally blurred to approximately ±11 km (0.1° in either direction). You can identify the body of water and country; you cannot reverse-engineer the precise parcel. This is part of how we protect the source data of every broker on the planet.

Can I add more islands?

v1 has 30 anonymized listings, static. If you encounter a real listing you'd like to see (publicly listed somewhere), email the broker link and I'll consider adding an anonymized version in a future update.

iCloud sync?

Not in v1. Your saved islands and bid history live on the device. v1.5 may add optional iCloud sync.

Is there a Mac / Watch / Vision Pro version?

No. This is designed as an iPhone-and-iPad couch-browsing experience. A luxury-asset catalogue on a productivity tool feels off-key.

Does it run on iOS 13 or older?

No. Minimum is iOS 14.0.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. All listings ship inside the app. The map is Apple's MapKit (the system caches map tiles on first use; subsequent offline use is fine).
