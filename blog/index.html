  • 一次 Linux bcache 写入卡顿 1 秒的深度排查 — 从用户感知到 root btree 锁竞争

    2026-06-08 · 中文 · English
    linux-kernel bcache ceph bpf rwsem performance

    生产 Ceph 集群在 bcache + HDD 配置下出现规律性"客户端写入 1-3 秒级延迟突刺"。经过 7 层证据排查(含 4 次假说证伪),最终定位到 cache_set->root btree 节点的 rw_semaphore 锁竞争,通过 BPF + 反汇编双重证明。完整记录排查路径与方法论。

    A production Ceph cluster on bcache + HDD hit reproducible 1-3 second write stalls. Through 7 layers of evidence (including 4 falsified hypotheses), the cause was localized to rw_semaphore contention on the cache_set->root btree node — proven by both BPF and disassembly. Full investigation path and methodology.
