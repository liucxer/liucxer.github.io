Frequently asked questions

Where is the app icon?

4,680 Weeks runs in the menu bar only, by design. After launch, look at the top-right of your screen for a small W 1,840 / 4,680 text indicator. That's the app. Click it for the popover.

How do I quit the app?

Click the menu bar icon → click Quit in the popover (bottom-right).

How do I open the main window?

Click the menu bar icon → click Open 4,680 Weeks in the popover.

How do I change my birthday after onboarding?

Menu bar icon → Settings… → General tab → adjust the date picker. Changes apply instantly.

How do I change my life expectancy?

Same place — Settings → General → use the stepper next to "Life expectancy".

How do I edit life stages?

Settings → Stages tab. You can:

  • Drag rows to reorder (later stages override earlier ones for overlapping ages)
  • Click the color swatch to change a stage's color
  • Edit name and age range inline
  • + Add Stage to add a new one
  • Minus icon to delete
  • Reset to defaults link restores the original 5

How do I enable the weekly Monday reminder?

Settings → Notifications tab → toggle on. macOS will ask for notification permission the first time.

How do I reset everything and re-do onboarding?

Settings → General → Reset Onboarding… → Confirm. Your custom life stages are kept; only the birthday and life expectancy are cleared. The welcome screen opens again.

Is there an iPhone or Apple Watch version?

Not yet. v1.1 will add an iOS app, Lock Screen widget, and Apple Watch complication. Universal Purchase, so buying the Mac version will unlock iOS automatically when v1.1 ships.

Why doesn't this work on macOS 13 or earlier?

4,680 Weeks needs macOS Sonoma 14.0+ for SwiftData and a few SwiftUI APIs. Older macOS is not supported.
