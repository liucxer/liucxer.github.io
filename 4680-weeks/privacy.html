Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-07

4,680 Weeks does not collect any data. Period. No exceptions.

Specifically

  • Your birthday, your life expectancy, and your life stages are stored exclusively on your Mac, in a standard Apple-managed local SwiftData store. They never leave your device.
  • The app contains no analytics frameworks (no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Sentry, no Crashlytics).
  • The app contains no advertising frameworks.
  • The app contains no third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • The app does not contact any server we operate.

Optional notifications

If you enable the weekly Monday-morning reminder in Settings, the app schedules local notifications via Apple's UserNotifications framework. These notifications are scheduled and fired entirely on your Mac; nothing about them is transmitted off-device.

iCloud

4,680 Weeks v1.0 does not use iCloud. A future version (v1.1) may add opt-in iCloud sync for settings only; if and when that ships, this page will be updated.

Apple's policies still apply

Like any Mac App Store purchase, your acquisition of 4,680 Weeks is governed by Apple's privacy policy and Mac App Store terms. The developer (Changxi Liu) does not have visibility into who has purchased the app — only aggregate sales counts.

Children

4,680 Weeks is rated 4+ and is safe for users of all ages. We collect no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices ever change (for example, if v1.1 adds opt-in iCloud sync), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's "What's New" release notes.
