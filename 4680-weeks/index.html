4,680 Weeks

A calendar for the rest of your life.

In 2014, Tim Urban drew a grid — 90 years of human life, one square per week. 4,680 squares total. He called it Your Life in Weeks and described those weeks as the most precious diamonds worth visualizing.

4,680 Weeks brings that grid to your Mac menu bar.

Pay once. No subscription. No tracking. Built for macOS Sonoma and later.

What you get

📅
Every week is one square The full grid lives in one window. Past weeks are filled with color. The current week pulses softly in gold. Future weeks wait, outlined.
🎨
Color your own chapters Default stages — childhood, school, university, career, retirement — tint your past automatically. Add your own: the two years you spent in Berlin, the months you wrote your dissertation. Any color you want.
🧭
Always one click away A small menu-bar indicator shows your current week count. Click for a popover with stats — weeks lived, weeks remaining, percentage elapsed.
🔔
Optional weekly reminder A quiet Monday-morning notification with this week's number. Easy to ignore, hard to forget. Off by default.
👁
Hover to remember Mouse over any square to see the date range, your age that week, and the life stage it belongs to.

What you don't get

  • No subscription. Pay once, use forever.
  • No account. No sign-up, no email harvest.
  • No tracking. Zero analytics, zero ads, zero telemetry.
  • No cloud. Everything stays on your Mac.
  • No upsells. What you buy is what you get.
